What Chaser takes off your plate

Reminders in the app your customers actually open

Send over WhatsApp, email or both. Write the message once in your own words and Chaser fills in the name, amount and pay link.

A schedule you control

Nudge 3 days before, on the due date, then every week. Skip weekends and festival days.

Stops when paid

Mark an invoice paid, or connect your UPI account, and reminders end immediately.

See who pays late, and by how much

A weekly summary shows your slowest customers and how many days faster invoices are paid since you started.

Set it up over a cup of tea

Most owners are sending their first reminder within fifteen minutes. There is nothing to install.

  1. Bring in your invoices

    Upload a CSV from Tally or Zoho, or add them by hand.

  2. Pick a reminder style

    Choose gentle, firm or your own wording, and set the schedule.

  3. Let it run

    Chaser sends, tracks replies and tells you when money arrives.
